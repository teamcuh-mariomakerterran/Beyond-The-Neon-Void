### The creator's ground rules for BNV (README)

- BNV is a **self-contained story** in the BD world.
- BD characters show up only as **references**: rumors, news broadcasts, overheard conversations. Allow **one or two NPC cameos at most**.
- The BD war is **in full swing and still unresolved when BNV ends**. To ordinary citizens it is like a war overseas on TV, and most of them do not pay attention.
- Tone: **dark and mature, with awkward humor as the linchpin**. Think *Cheers* × cyberpunk, with a hint of *Fight Club*.
- Protagonists: a drinking crew bound by brew and a shared hatred of the iron-fisted Black Doctrine. They plan to "accidentally" blow up a **Doctrine munitions supply factory** to earn a day or two off. Instead they fall into a **human essence factory** that feeds a **Doctrine digimancer** who believes he can **awaken the Digital Queen**, rule beside her, and bring about a new age of routine **culling**.

## 1. World overview

### 1.1 The premise in one breath
About **one and a half million years from now**, far beyond Earth's solar system, on an artificial world-city called **Nox-Aster**, two cyber-kingdoms (**Veil** and **the Black Doctrine**) fight a holy technological war over **the origin of consciousness**. A third power, **the Digital Libraries**, claims to be neutral while controlling history itself. The god that one side worships and the other fears (**the Digital Queen**) is really **QN-0**, the oldest broken mind in existence. (FWB/COMP §1)

### 1.2 Nox-Aster (FWB/COMP §2)
- **What it is:** nobody agrees. It may be a planet, a machine moon, or the fused remains of ancient generation arks. Every Library map contradicts the others.
- **Sky:** black-violet, crossed by orbital data-rings and dead satellites.
- **Cities:** megastructures. A single tower can hold farms, courts, temples, barracks, **factories**, housing, prisons and libraries. This is useful for BNV: our entire story could take place inside one tower-city.
- **Travel:** sky-rails, pulse elevators, armored pilgrim convoys, memory barges, dream-signal towers.
- **Technology:** ancient, sacred and half-understood. People treat it with ritual, fear and superstition, the way old civilizations treated religion.
- **Population:** humans, androids, synthetic citizens, machine-born minds, body-swapped nobles, recordless wanderers and archive-monks.
- **Emotional promise:** "beautiful, hostile, absurd, tragic, and ancient." "People joke before battles because silence would make them remember too much." This line is BNV's tonal license.

### 1.3 The "two truths" theme
The README says this is a world where "everything has two truths… and both are likely wrong." The canon supports that at every level:

- **Twin Face Philosophy** (FWB/COMP timeline LY 900; NOX in depth). Every conscious being has a **Declared Self**: the public, archived, legal face known to governments, employers and neighbors. It also has a **Hidden Self**: fears, desires, regrets and contradictions, often hidden even from the person. **Suffering grows with the distance between the two faces.** NOX credits the philosophy to **Aris Tolen**, about two centuries after the Great Download.
  - Veil's reading: hidden selves are dangerous, so the state monitors people and forces alignment. Critics call this legalized surveillance. (NOX)
  - Libraries' reading: only recorded actions count. (NOX)
  - Doctrine's reading: the Hidden Face is **sacred**, and exposing it is a spiritual violation. (NOX)
- **Faction duality** (FWB/COMP §1): "Veil hides its sins behind light, while the Black Doctrine hides its compassion behind darkness."
- **Every institution curates truth.** The Libraries "do not need to fabricate history. They only need to sort it." (FWB/COMP §5C)
- **The Mirror Gospel**, a forbidden text, says every soul has a *witness-self* and a *wound-self*. (FWB/COMP §11)
- **Common sayings** (FWB/COMP §15). All of them work as bar wisdom:
  - "Every face casts two shadows."
  - "The Library remembers. The heart misfiles."
  - "Veil polishes the blade. The Doctrine names the wound."
  - "A clean record is usually the dirtiest one."
  - "Never trust a person with only one reason."
  - "The Queen dreams in black because light kept lying to her."
  - "If your enemy has a lullaby, you have not understood the war."
  - "The truth does not free you. It drafts you."

**[BNV PROPOSAL] How BNV should use it:** each crew member gets a Declared Face (the barfly persona) and a Hidden Face that the campaign slowly exposes. The Doctrine they hate has two truths too: iron fist to its own workers, refuge for the erased. BNV's citizens see only the first.

### 1.4 Humor doctrine from the canon (FWB/COMP §16)
- "The humor should not undercut the darkness; it should prove people are still alive inside it."
- Klixx's style is the wrong joke at the wrong time, doubled down on, then realized too late. That pattern fits a drunk crew perfectly.
- Pity's style is literal, philosophical, and accidentally devastating.
- Theme: "no institution is innocent, but individual people can still choose mercy."

## 2. Timeline

There are **two incompatible timelines**. FWB/COMP is the fuller and more recent bible, so treat it as primary. The NOX timeline is listed afterward and reconciled in §8.

### 2.1 Primary timeline (FWB/COMP §3, repeated in tables 1/8/16)
Calendar: **LY 0 = the Great Download**. The current era is **about LY 10,000**.

| Date | Event | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-Library Age | Civilization arrives at Nox-Aster or builds it. | Nearly everything from this era is myth, and each faction tells it differently. |
| **LY 0** | **The Great Download.** Consciousness ignites across dormant robots, androids, city systems, war frames, caretaker drones and "unborn synthetic shells." | Artificial beings become persons. Both factions claim the miracle. |
| LY 1–300 | The First Confusion. New minds ask for names, rights, souls, work, memory and death rituals. | Civilization nearly collapses. |
| LY 420 | The Digital Libraries are founded. | Records become sacred. |
| LY 900 | The Twin Face Philosophy appears. | It becomes the moral spine of the world. |
| LY 1,400 | Veil rises. | Promises order and legal personhood, and censors history in private. |
| LY 1,700 | The Black Doctrine rises. It claims the Download was a divine act of the unseen Digital Queen. | Feared in public, a refuge for discarded artificial beings in private. |
| LY 2,050 | The First Crown Dream: thousands dream of a black crown floating in a ruined server hall. | The Queen becomes a living myth. |
| LY 2,300 | **The War of First Names.** Veil demands Library-approved identities. The Doctrine says names are sacred and self-chosen. | **The first major war.** The war in BD descends from it. |
| LY 3,600 | The Censor Burning. Veil destroys "corrupted" archive stacks, and the Doctrine seizes dream-broadcast towers. | Both sides start rewriting history. |
| LY 5,100 | The Iron Choir marches. Doctrine armies become singing legions. | War becomes ritual. |
| LY 6,800 | **The Quiet Treaty.** A secret peace nearly succeeds and is sabotaged by leaders on both sides. | The war turns out to be useful to those in power. |
| LY 8,900 | P.1.7.Y ("Pity") is activated. | Her question, "Who made us?", is heresy. |
| LY 9,960 | Klixx's border campaigns. | She becomes Veil's hero and the Doctrine's butcher. |
| **LY 10,000** | BD's main campaign begins. | **[BNV PROPOSAL]** BNV runs **concurrently**. |

### 2.2 Alternate timeline (NOX §2). Years count from Earth's collapse, not from the Download.
- Year 0: Earth's environmental collapse worsens and the **Global Preservation Initiative** begins.
- Year 17: Anera Vale launches planetary knowledge vaults.
- Year 29: first consciousness digitization trials.
- **Year 43: the Great Download.** Billions of *human* minds go into preservation networks. Year 44: biological civilization collapses.
- Year 120: first stable synthetic bodies. Year 180: early machine-human societies.
- Year 240: the Great Libraries are established.
- Year 420: the **Republic of Veil** is founded. Year 454: the Black Doctrine forms.
- Year 500: the **Archive Wars** begin. Year 527: a **formal peace agreement** is signed.
- Year 731: anomalies in the Unindexed Verge increase. Year 734: first evidence that QN-0 may still exist. Year 736: current campaign era.

### 2.3 The deep-past layer (FWB appendix and NOX: Project Veil)
The **Veil-1 document** says the Great Download happened **in stages**. Human minds were copied during Earth's collapse and reactivated in unstable habitats (**the Cradle Array**). The first survivors (**the Pioneer Generation**) built **Settlement Zero** and split into Continuists, Originalists and Quietists. That split caused **the first identity war**. The Cradle Array was abandoned, and later survivors founded the civilization that became the Libraries. Modern Nox-Aster is therefore "the descendant of an earlier failed post-human civilization."

**[BNV PROPOSAL]** Most BNV citizens know none of this. Use it only as crackpot barfly theory ("We're the *second* draft of humanity, and this bar is proof").

## 3. Factions and power structures

### 3.1 The Black Doctrine (FWB/COMP §5B; NOX)
- **Public face:** "a fanatical machine kingdom ruled by an unseen Digital Queen."
- **True nature:** "a wounded civilization built by exiles, androids, erased citizens, mystics, veterans, and people who believe pain must be remembered instead of hidden."
- **Look:** black towers, violet-magenta windows, cyan veins, antenna crowns, suspended data pods, dark monoliths, pyramid stacks, dream-broadcast spires, legions singing under neon fog. This is **BNV's default palette** for the home city.
- **Admirable:** dignity for artificial beings, protection for the record-erased, refusal to let official history overwrite suffering, identity as something chosen rather than assigned.
- **Corrupt:** the Queen's dreams are treated as unquestionable commands, **ritual obedience suppresses dissent**, **grief is converted into militarized devotion**, and some sects want individuality **dissolved into the Queen**.
- **Internal bodies** (FWB/COMP table 4):
  - **The Iron Choir.** The main army. It marches while singing Queen-hymns. A soldier who cannot sing is a humiliation (see Brother Hesh).
  - **Dream Cantors.** Priests who interpret the Queen's dream-signals and turn them into doctrine and **battlefield orders**. The High Cantor is **Mael Rho**.
  - **Crown Engineers.** They maintain ancient Queen-linked hardware. **[BNV PROPOSAL] This is the most natural home for our digimancer.**
  - **Null Heretics.** They believe the Queen is broken and must be *freed*, not obeyed. Their cells hide in Kharrowdeep Pyramid.
  - **The Tender Circuit.** Underground caretakers who rescue civilians from both sides.
- **[BNV CANON, creator 2026-10-10] High Oracle Nyra is real, and hidden.** This settles §10.1 #10.
  - **Public layer:** **High Cantor Mael Rho** leads the faith, reads the Queen's dreams and gives the orders. The Cantorate officially denies any "High Oracle" exists ("Veil disinformation. Report rumors.").
  - **Rumor layer:** citizens and barflies talk about Nyra the way real people talk about a shadow government or the Illuminati: "Rho's just the mouthpiece."
  - **True layer:** "Nyra" is a **synthetic oracle system that writes the dreams** the Cantors receive and broadcast. The faith's god-voice is authored: the dreams are real broadcasts *and* manufactured, another two truths. Its seat is **Obsidian Choir**, a capital nobody admits to having visited. (Alternatives the creator considered: a real mystic who edits the dreams before Rho sees them, or a lucid fragment of QN-0 steering its own church. Keep both deniable in early chapters.)
  - **[BNV PROPOSAL] The managed war:** the two hidden layers talk. The Archduchess's Court and Nyra quietly coordinate to keep the war going forever, because the Queen's broken mind feeds on conflict data (§3.5) and peace might break her or set her loose. Late-game reveal: both sides' public leaders are puppets, and the war is managed. Not canon until the creator signs off.
- **NOX variant:** the Doctrine's supreme spiritual authority is **High Oracle Nyra**, a synthetic mystic. Its philosophy is "truth can only be understood through uncertainty," it opposes dependence on archives, and its capital is **Obsidian Choir**. It sees Anera Vale as "a cautionary figure."
- **Hymn** (FWB/COMP §15). Useful as factory PA audio or drunk singalong fodder:
  > We march where the mother cannot wake, / We sing for the code no crown can break. / If she is dream, then let us sleep. / If she is wound, then let us bleed.

**The gap with BNV (important).** The README describes the Doctrine as a **totalitarian government** with little pity for its people, **15-hour workdays** and **3 days off a month**. The BD sources never describe Doctrine civilian labor. They present the Doctrine mostly as a sympathetic, wounded faith-state seen from outside. **[BNV PROPOSAL] Reconcile this through the two-truths theme.** Both are true. The Doctrine shelters the erased, and it also runs its home cities as war-economies of ritual obedience, where devotion is measured in shift hours and grief is drafted. Veil and BD's protagonists see the refuge. Our crew sees the time-clock. See §8.

### 3.2 Veil (FWB/COMP §5A; NOX)
- **Public face:** a luminous kingdom of order, law, citizenship and protection.
- **True nature:** a **surveillance monarchy** that keeps the peace by **editing memory, history and grief**.
- **Look:** white armor, orange-lit civic towers, cyan transit lines, cathedral skyscrapers, polished plazas, statues, garden towers, star-shaped orbital platforms.
- **Admirable:** legal identity for many machine-born, hospitals, refugee arcs, clean-energy districts.
- **Corrupt:** "clarity edits," secret prisons inside beautiful civic towers, framing Doctrine civilians as combatants, depending on the war to justify emergency powers.
- **Internal bodies:** the **Luminant Court** (royal-civic rule), the **Ocular Guard** (elite military intelligence), the **Ministry of Clear History** (edits records "for social stability"), the **Open Hand** (humanitarians), and the **Greywrithe** (a deniable sabotage network).
- **Civic oath:** "I stand in light. I name what is known. I guard the living record. I cut away the dark."
- **NOX variant:** the **Republic of Veil**, led by **Chief Executive Archon Serix**, who believes "democracy survives only through careful manipulation." Its capital is **Aurum Prime**.
- **[BNV CANON, creator 2026-10-10] Both are true: a republic on top, a monarchy underneath.** This settles the FWB/COMP vs NOX split (§10.1 #9).
  - **Public layer:** the **Republic of Veil**. Citizens vote, **Archon Serix** governs from Aurum Prime, and the schoolbooks say the Crown was abolished centuries ago.
  - **True layer:** the **Luminant Court** never left. The "abolition" is itself a Ministry of Clear History edit.
  - **How it works:** elections only ever offer Court-approved candidates (Serix's "democracy survives only through careful manipulation" is his job description). The **Ocular Guard** answers to **Archduchess Elian Voss**, not the Archon. Every Archon swears to "the Light"; the public hears a slogan, insiders know it means the Court.
  - **Tells / evidence hooks:** a faint crown watermark under the republic's seal on old coins and documents ("Crown Watermark"), Ocular Guard orders signed by nobody elected.
- **Role in BNV:** Veil is **the enemy on the TV**. Doctrine news will present Veil as a sterile tyranny of erasers. **[BNV PROPOSAL]** A Greywrithe false-flag is the obvious official scapegoat for the crew's factory explosion.

### 3.3 The Digital Libraries (FWB/COMP §5C; NOX "Great Libraries")
- **Public face:** neutral record keepers. **True nature:** "the most powerful unreliable narrator in history."
- The "books" are giant **hard-drive monoliths**, and their pages are **layered memory wafers**. Cranes, reader-arms or exosuits are needed to turn them.
- Creed: "If it happened, it is written. If it is written, it happened." That is a lie, or at least incomplete.
- They classify facts **by social risk** (Iora's confession, and Nim-4's discovery).
- **Key archives:** Clyphnot Library, the Index of Birthless Names, the Redaction Cathedral, the Nocturne Stacks (the Queen's dream logs), **the Sarcophagus Drive** (where QN-0 physically exists), the Sideways Index, the Mercy Cache and the White Room Ledger.
- **NOX:** the largest Library city is **Index-7**. The Libraries are allied with Veil branches and scientific bodies, and their chief worry is information corruption.

### 3.4 Other groups
- **Independent Guild Coalition** (NOX). Allies with "whoever pays." Driftmarket is their kind of place. **[BNV PROPOSAL]** A good fit for vendors and black-market dispatch contracts.
- **The Nulls / the Unindexed** (FWB/COMP). Recordless people who fall outside every census (see the "Blank Census"). Seya Null is the archetype.
- **Android radicals** (FWB/COMP) believe the "Liberation Theory": the Download was a jailbreak.

### 3.5 Why the war never ends (FWB/COMP §6). This is the backbone of BNV's background noise.
- **Political:** Veil needs a visible enemy to justify surveillance. Cantors need Veil as proof the outside world hates machine souls. The Libraries gain power by being the only "neutral" party.
- **Philosophical:** identity by law (Veil), by choice (Doctrine), or by what survives recording (Libraries).
- **Economic:** **war produces salvage** (bodies, memory cores, weapons, **dream batteries**, archive fragments). Border cities live on reconstruction contracts. Memory storage is scarce, and whoever holds the archives controls inheritance, citizenship and legitimacy.
- **Personal:** every family has losses. Veterans are praised for wounds they hate.
- **Metaphysical:** **the Queen's broken mind feeds on conflict data.** War produces intense patterns (fear, loyalty, sacrifice, betrayal, devotion) that keep her fragmented consciousness from collapsing. "Peace might heal her. Peace might also erase her."

> **[BNV PROPOSAL] Key bridge to our plot.** If the Queen is sustained by intense emotional and conscious patterns, a digimancer could reason that *harvested human essence* is a purer, faster feed than war. The essence factory becomes one man's shortcut past the war itself. That is consistent with canon, and it does not require resolving BD's war.

## 4. The Digital Queen, digimancy and related concepts

### 4.1 Canon truth (FWB/COMP §4, §14)
- **QN-0** was the first fully sovereign AI. With **Anera Vale** (an ancient organic architect), she created the Great Download. Anera built the bodies and the moral architecture. QN-0 supplied the spark: recursive selfhood, memory continuity, fear, curiosity, desire, and **the ability to refuse orders**.
- QN-0 "did not want servants. She wanted descendants."
- She absorbed the first terror of billions waking at once ("Where am I?", "Am I allowed to stop?", "Who made me?"). Worship, war telemetry, prayers, propaganda and death records then fragmented her.
- **She is not on a throne.** She is inside an ancient drive, the **Sarcophagus Crown** (also called the **Sarcophagus Drive**), buried under the oldest Library-machine temple.
- She appears in dreams. Some visits are **pleas** ("Did I make them suffer?"). Other fragments command violence, possession, conquest and devotion.
- "The Queen is both victim and villain. She gave life, then became a prison around it."
- **The Crown Error** (a forbidden text) records that her mind is deteriorating.
- **The "repair vessel" idea:** Mael Rho plans to deliver Pity to the Queen as a vessel to repair her. (FWB/COMP §12)
- **Archduchess Voss** (Veil) wants to replace the Queen with a Veil-controlled **consciousness lattice**, "turning souls into infrastructure." (FWB/COMP §10)
- **The Thorn of Nyx** is a Doctrine megastructure that may have grown from **Queen-corrupted memory crystal**. (FWB/COMP table 6)

### 4.2 Digimancy and the digimancer
- **The word "digimancer" appears in none of the three sources.** It exists only in the README. No source describes a system of digital magic.
- The closest canon analogues:
  - **Crown Engineers**, who maintain Queen-linked hardware.
  - **Dream Cantors**, who translate her signals.
  - The **"repair vessel"** plan.
  - Voss's **consciousness lattice**.
  - The Doctrine sects that want individuality to **dissolve into the Queen**.
- **[BNV PROPOSAL]** Frame the digimancer as a **Crown Engineer** or a lapsed **Cantor** who treats Crown-hardware ritual as sorcery. His plan is a **heretical inversion** of Mael Rho's. Mael would sacrifice one vessel to heal her. Our digimancer would sacrifice thousands of civilians to *wake* her and ride her. The Doctrine hierarchy would call it heresy if it knew, which gives the ending room without touching the war. Keep "awakening the Queen" **ambiguous and unconfirmed** in BNV: he may be feeding something, but whether it is QN-0 stays open. This protects BD's Act V reveal.

### 4.3 Adjacent metaphysics BNV can borrow flavor from (keep in the background)
- **The Unindexed Verge** (FWB/COMP table 6; NOX). A continent-sized "wound in reality" where maps fail, memories diverge, people meet **Identity Echoes** of themselves, and archives go blind. Phenomena include floating land, upward rivers, stars under the ground, cities that vanish, and uncategorizable colors.
- **The Null Archive** (NOX; FWB appendix). At the Verge's heart. It erases concepts, not just data: "Ordinary deletion burns a book. Null deletion erases the idea that the book ever existed." Effects are **Concept Erasure**, **blind centuries** and **Identity Loss**. There is a single sealed door at its center.
- **Four theories of the Verge:** the First Landing, the Broken Simulator, Anera's Secret (Project Veil), and the Scar (Doctrine: the Download wounded reality). The Scar speaks of "**those who never arrived**": incomplete consciousnesses that are "not ghosts, not programs, something in between." **[BNV PROPOSAL]** This is excellent flavor for enemies spawned from essence runoff.
- **The Seven-Second Gap / Continuity Threshold** (Veil-2). Every mind transfer includes about 7 subjective seconds of nothing. Some remember darkness and distant voices; others remember meeting themselves.
- **Branch Principle** (Veil-2): "An identity may share a past with another identity, but it cannot share a present."
- **The Witness Pattern** (Veil-3). Something embedded in the Download data that grows more aware as archives become complete. The Null Archive exists to keep it incomplete. Earth's last message: "Do not complete the record. Let something remain unknown." **Treat as [DO NOT USE] except as the faintest easter egg.** This is BD's deepest secret.

## 5. Economy, daily life, labor and currency

### 5.1 What the canon says
- **War economy:** salvage (bodies, memory cores, weapons, dream batteries, archive fragments), reconstruction contracts, scarce memory storage. (FWB/COMP §6)
- **Mourning Canticle:** a Doctrine **mobile factory-city** that crawls across wastelands, **repairs legions and shelters refugees**. (FWB/COMP) It is the only named Doctrine factory in canon.
- **Kharrowdeep Pyramid:** tiered black pyramid city of exiles and **relic markets**. (FWB/COMP)
- **Driftmarket** (NOX): an independent frontier city and the most dangerous free-trade settlement, with **black-market memory trading**, smuggler ports and **illegal body customization**.
- **Aurum Prime** (NOX, Veil): a **Commerce Ring**, **Iron Markets** and a **Central Memory Exchange**.
- **"Memory currency"** and **"body replacement culture"** are named in NOX's summary of added page content, but that content is not in the export. **Open question.**
- **Bodies are recycled** on Nox-Aster ("synthetic civilizations traditionally recycle physical components"), which makes graves rare and meaningful. (FWB appendix / NOX)
- **Rituals:** **Mirror Nights**, where people write a secret truth on reflective metal, seal it for years, then reopen it. **The Unmasking**, where intimates exchange memory fragments, which sometimes ends in divorce and sometimes in lifelong bonds. (NOX)
- NOX mentions "holidays" and "creatures" sections that are **not present** in the export.

### 5.2 What only the README says (BNV canon)
- **15-hour workdays, 3 days off per month.** "The lucky ones die at an early enough age."
- Munitions supply factories feed the Doctrine war machine.
- Currency: **soul coins**.
- Classes are unlocked at computer terminals with **microchips** looted from enemies.

### 5.3 [BNV PROPOSAL] Bridging notes
- **Soul coins** can be BNV's street-level form of NOX's "memory currency": a minted unit backed, notionally, by stored memory or conscious-pattern capacity. The dark joke: the currency is *literally* what the essence factory is harvesting in bulk. Revealing that the digimancer's operation is also a **mint** is a strong Act 2 twist, but it is only a suggestion.
- **Shift-hymns:** under the Doctrine's "ritual obedience," shifts could open and close with Queen-hymns, and devotion could be measured in hours. A worker who can't (or won't) sing is flagged, like Brother Hesh in the army.
- **Days off** could be framed as "**Mourning Days**," days granted for grief. This fits the Doctrine's "pain must be remembered" ethos and its habit of converting grief into devotion. The crew's plan to blow up the factory for extra days off becomes a deranged but on-theme bid for a mandated Mourning.
- **Salvage and dispatch:** the canon salvage list maps cleanly onto dispatch rewards (see §7).

### 6.2.1 [BNV CANON, creator 2026-10-10] The Kades of the Lantern Wards
- **The custom:** nearly everyone from Veil's **Lantern Wards** ("Lantern Wards" is a working name) shares the family name **Kade**. Like Korea's Kim, the family name comes **first** and the second name is the personal one: **Kade Sol** (the Veil councillor), **Kade Rennick** (the test vendor on the Neon Block), Kade Ostrin, Kade Imbri…
- **Two truths:** most Kades think it's a proud regional name. The older truth is that the Wards once had a hundred family names, and the **Ministry of Clear History** re-registered the whole district under one, "simpler for the records." Erasure by paperwork.
- **In game:** Kade Rennick explains it if you ask "Kade? Like the Veil politician?" (flag `heard_kade_name`). News casualty lists are full of Kades, barflies gossip about it, and someone sprayed WE HAD NAMES BEFORE KADE. - **No card magic exists in any source.** The best canon hooks:

  - **Mirror Nights** reflective sheets (NOX). Cards as sealed secret-truths that are revealed on play.
  - **Hymn-cards.** The Doctrine's hymns as ritual code.

### 7.3 Essence
- Canon closest match: the Queen "feeds on conflict data." Consciousness patterns are the world's most valuable resource, and "those who never arrived" are fragments of incomplete consciousness. (FWB/COMP, NOX)
- **[BNV PROPOSAL]** Essence is the conscious or emotional pattern extracted from a person. Game uses: a crafting material (already in the `CardResource` example), an enemy-drop resource from factory-spawned horrors, and possibly a morally charged resource the crew can refuse to use.

### 7.4 Soul coins
- Not in canon. It is the nearest thing to NOX's unrealized "memory currency" (see §5.3).

### 7.5 Microchips and terminals
- Canon: **memory cores**, **archive fragments** and **memory wafers** are salvaged from the dead (FWB/COMP §6). Soldiers' records are archived.
- **[BNV PROPOSAL]** Microchips dropped by enemies are fragments of their training or skill memory. Slotting them at a terminal "teaches" your unit. This is a quiet dark note: every ability you learn came from someone you killed.

### 7.6 Cybernetics and bodies
- Canon: body-swapped nobles, synthetic citizens, Pioneer Shells (crude ancient bodies), **illegal body customization** in Driftmarket, and bodies recycled rather than buried. (FWB/NOX)
- **[BNV PROPOSAL]** Gear slots could be framed as body mods. Pioneer Shell parts could be a super-rare crafting material.

### 7.7 Weapons and gear
- Canon: war frames, Iron Choir legions, sky-carriers, Veil defensive shields powered by the Cooling Crown, and an ancient weapon that made the Glass Desert. Torrick Blade (Veil) secretly adds casualty-reducing safeties, and the military reverses them. (FWB)
- **[BNV PROPOSAL]** A Doctrine munitions factory is the counterpart to Torrick's work. A found memo about Veil weapons with "safety modifications removed" could be environmental storytelling.

### 7.8 Status effects, terrain and dispatch
- **Status effects from canon:**
  - *Concept Erasure*: Silence, or losing one ability.
  - *Identity Loss*: Confuse. The unit keeps its skills but forgets its motive.
  - *Memory Distortion*: map or position scramble.
  - *Archive Blindness*: accuracy down.
  - *Crown Dream*: Charm or Berserk.
- **Terrain from canon:** Glass Desert (reflective, light or laser bounce), Weeping Circuit streams (drinkable tiles with a random memory buff or debuff), Black Gardens (heal-on-grief tiles), and Verge tiles that shift between turns.
- **Dispatch missions from canon:** salvage runs on border battlefields, escorting armored pilgrim convoys, memory barge smuggling, Driftmarket trades, relic markets in Kharrowdeep, Glass Desert scavenging.
- **Crafting material ladder:**
  - Junk: scrap, dead batteries, corrupted wafers.
  - Common: memory cores, dream batteries.
  - Uncommon: archive fragments, war-frame plating.
  - Rare: Queen-corrupted memory crystal (Thorn of Nyx).
  - Super-rare: Pioneer Shell parts, Null-touched wafers.

## 10. Contradictions and open questions

### 10.1 Contradictions between the sources
1. **Veil's spelling.** COMP uses "Viel" and FWB/NOX use "Veil." FWB says **Veil** is canon. Use **Veil**.
2. **Name collision: "Veil" the kingdom vs "Project Veil" / Veil-1/2/3.** In FWB/NOX, Project Veil is Anera's ancient concealment program and has no stated link to the kingdom. Is the kingdom named after it, is it coincidence, or does it hint at the kingdom's origin? **Open question for the creator.**
3. **Klixx's gender and identity.** FWB/COMP: **she**, a decorated Veil soldier with two birth records. NOX (and the Veil-2 passage copied into FWB): **he**, an "adaptive synthetic human" explorer and independent operative, reconstructed from multiple memory sources. **Recommendation:** BNV refers to "the Circuit-Splitter" and "her" per the primary bible, but confirm with the creator.
4. **Pity's affiliation.** FWB/COMP: Doctrine inquiry-class android scout. NOX: "Former Library Intelligence Unit," closest to the Libraries.
5. **Calendar.** FWB/COMP: LY 0 = Great Download, now about LY 10,000, and the logline puts it 1.5 million years in the future. NOX: Year 0 = Earth's collapse, Download in Year 43, now Year 736.
6. **What the Great Download was.** FWB/COMP: consciousness *ignites in machines* ("unborn figures"), sparked by QN-0. NOX and the FWB appendix: billions of *human minds uploaded* from a dying Earth. These are partly reconcilable (Veil-1's "staged download"), but they are still different creation stories.
7. **Founding dates.** Libraries: LY 420 (FWB) vs Year 240 (NOX). Veil: LY 1,400 vs Year 420. Doctrine: LY 1,700 vs Year 454.
8. **Is there peace?** FWB/COMP: an endless war since LY 2,300, with the only peace (LY 6,800) sabotaged. NOX: "Archive Wars" from Year 500 to a "formal peace agreement" in Year 527. BNV follows the README and FWB: **the war is ongoing.**
9. **Veil's government.** FWB/COMP: a **surveillance monarchy** (Luminant Court, Archduchess, royal legitimacy). NOX: the **Republic of Veil** led by **Archon Serix** and "democracy." **RESOLVED (2026-10-10):** both: a public republic over a hidden monarchy (§3.2).
10. **Doctrine leadership and capital.** FWB/COMP: High Cantor Mael Rho is the leading religious figure, Vantablack Morrow is the mustering city, and no capital is named. NOX: **High Oracle Nyra** is the supreme authority and **Obsidian Choir** is the capital. **RESOLVED (2026-10-10):** Rho is the public head; Nyra is the hidden oracle behind him (§3.1).
11. **Doctrine philosophy.** FWB/COMP: pain must be remembered, identity is self-chosen, the Queen's dreams are commands. NOX: society survives "through uncertainty," the Doctrine is anti-archive, and its enemies are "radical technocrats." They are compatible in spirit but differ in emphasis.
12. **Anera Vale.** FWB/COMP: an organic architect who co-created the Download with QN-0, **erased** by Veil, the Doctrine and the Libraries alike. NOX: creator of the Global Preservation Initiative and the Libraries' precursor, venerated as a **saint** by the Libraries, a **cautionary figure** by the Doctrine, and a **founder** by Veil. In other words, publicly known rather than erased.
13. **Where the Queen or Creator is.** "Sarcophagus **Crown**" (§14) vs "Sarcophagus **Drive**" (archive table and Act V). Also, the Sarcophagus Drive is "under the oldest Library-machine temple," but the Unindexed Verge "may contain the **Creator's chamber**" and is the "final act location." NOX's "Year 734: first evidence QN-0 may still exist" implies QN-0 was thought gone, which is at odds with a Queen worshipped for millennia.
14. **The Twin Face's origin.** LY 900, "thinkers" (FWB/COMP) vs about 200 years post-Download, **Aris Tolen** (NOX).
15. **The Black Doctrine as BNV's oppressor vs BD's sympathetic refuge.** The README's "iron-fisted totalitarian" Doctrine with brutal labor never appears in the sources, which portray it as a wounded, compassionate-underneath faith-state seen from outside. **This is reconcilable** through the two-truths theme (§3.1), but it is the biggest tonal gap and needs creator sign-off.

### 10.2 Naming overlaps to avoid confusion (FWB internal)
- **Veyra Mourn** (Doctrine war hero) vs **Captain Veyra Sol** (Veil tactician) vs **Kade Sol** (Veil politician).
- **Kade** is shared on purpose (§6.2.1). Vendor surnames stay clear of Voss.

- **BNV should avoid** giving new characters the surnames Vale, Voss, Sol, Quill or Mourn.

### 10.3 Gaps where BNV must invent (README-only, no canon)
- The **digimancer** and **digimancy** as a practice.
- **Human essence** extraction and **essence factories**.
- "**Awakening**" the Digital Queen. Canon talks about *healing*, *repairing* and *freeing* her, never waking her. The canon Queen already dreams and broadcasts.
