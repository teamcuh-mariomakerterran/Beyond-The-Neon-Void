# Vault Breach: the model mission (`demo_vault_breach` on `vault_breach_demo`)

Generated by `tools/demo/make_tactics_demo.py`. Change the generator, not the JSON. It is the best example in the repo of a mission whose **objective is a tactics puzzle, hinted by the world, echoed by the news.** Copy its structure.

## Why it works (rules to reuse)
1. **One verb per anchor, readable from the glyph:** terminal = HACK, switch = FLIP, device = ACTIVATE, intel = DOWNLOAD (`Interactions.KINDS`).
2. **The boss is gated by the map, not by HP.** Shield, cloak or door states create a plan: "who goes for the relays while who holds the line".
3. **Spread objectives** pull the party apart against the CT clock (each anchor costs `ap: 1`).
4. **The world tells you the answer** (NPC evidence; Brian's ROADMAP rule: "Propaganda works through NPC evidence").
5. **News echoes your action** (`news` action → `EventBus.broadcast_line("news", …)` → signage screens and hub ticker).
6. **Optional greed** (cache, hidden loot) rewards risk without blocking the win.
7. **Failure stings, but doesn't hard-lock:** wrong sequence order = 10% damage + reset.

## Gaps to know
- The AI doesn't use anchors (enemies never re-shield or close doors), so puzzle pressure comes only from enemy damage and the CT clock. See `tactics-ai`.
- `find_loot` quest objectives never progress for anchors (anchors emit no `loot_discovered`). To make "take the vault cache" a quest step, give the anchor a `flag` action (it already sets `vault_cache_taken`) and use objective `{type: "flag", target: "vault_cache_taken"}`.
