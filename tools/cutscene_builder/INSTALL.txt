# Cutscene Builder (Black Doctrine)

Open `cutscene-builder.html` in a browser.

Scenes live in `cutscenes/` (`.parallax.json`).
Open → pick a file, or Proj → Import scene pack (multi JSON).

Docs: PLAYBOOK.md, STATUS.md, cutscene-builder-guide.html
Templates: templates/

Asset library is not bundled here — scenes embed what they need.
Working asset copies stay on the box under /workspace/bd-tools/cutscene/assets/.
