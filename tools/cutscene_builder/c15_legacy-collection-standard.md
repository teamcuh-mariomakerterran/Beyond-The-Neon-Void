# Legacy Collection Standard

- **id:** `c15`
- **section:** discoveries
- **class:** Collection Template
- **status:** Template
- **impact:** High

A complete collection must unlock narrative transformation rather than ordinary loot.

## Production notes

- Define 6–12 discoverable fragments with distinct locations and perspectives.
- Each fragment provides a small scene, line, or annotation rather than raw exposition.
- Completion unlocks one prestige cinematic, at least one companion interaction, and one persistent archive change.
- Collections linked to the Hidden Ending should never announce that connection directly.
