# Visual Motif Standard

- **id:** `c19`
- **section:** production
- **class:** Art Direction
- **status:** Foundation
- **impact:** High

Cinematic motifs identify each institution before dialogue explains anything.

## Production notes

- Viel — white geometry, orange civic light, cyan transit lines, clean wipes, controlled camera paths.
- Black Doctrine — black silhouettes, magenta windows, cyan signal veins, hymn-wave overlays, vertical compositions.
- Libraries — pale institutional light, annotation layers, page-like wipes, neutral framing that becomes threatening.
- Verge — mismatched parallax, duplicated layers, impossible scale, delayed shadows, broken transitions.
- QN-0 — black crown geometry, soft voice fragments, signal bloom, contradictory color temperatures.
