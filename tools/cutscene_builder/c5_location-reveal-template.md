# Location Reveal Template

- **id:** `c5`
- **section:** templates
- **class:** Environment Introduction
- **status:** Template
- **impact:** Medium

A restrained four-shot arrival sequence for major regions of Nox-Aster.

## Production notes

- Shot 1 — Approach: layered skyline, slow drift, long fade, near-to-far parallax.
- Shot 2 — Environmental behavior: trains, signal lights, prayer screens, archive cranes, or refugee flow.
- Shot 3 — Name card using {PLACE} and {REGION}; text fades in at staggered times.
- Shot 4 — Character enters or watches from the foreground; letterbox remains consistent.
- Recommended runtime: 12–24 seconds. The location should make a political argument before dialogue begins.
